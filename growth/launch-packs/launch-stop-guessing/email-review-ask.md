# Email: review ask

**Send:** T+21
**To:** segment who purchased in launch week (T0-T+6)
**Rule that overrides enthusiasm:** never offer anything in exchange for a
review, never name a star value. Amazon filters and sometimes penalizes
incentivized or solicited-for-stars reviews, and it reads as exactly what
it is.

**Subject line options:**
- A. `Did Stop Guessing hold up once you used it?`
- B. `One honest question about the book`

---

Hi [First name],

You picked up Stop Guessing Whether It Works a few weeks ago. I'm not
going to ask if you liked it, I'm going to ask something more specific:
did the golden-set method or the cost model actually change how you
argued for or against something at work?

If the answer is yes, or even "sort of, here's where it didn't fit," a
review that says that specific thing is worth more to the next reader
than five stars with no detail. If the answer is no, a review that says
where it fell short is just as useful, to me and to them.
