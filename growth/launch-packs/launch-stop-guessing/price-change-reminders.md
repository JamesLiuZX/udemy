# Price-change reminders (author checklist, not customer-facing)

These are operational reminders for the KDP dashboard, not copy to send
anyone. Cross-reference against `docs/05-kdp-playbook.md` §3's pricing
ladder: $0.99 launch week, then $4.99, then $9.99 once 25+ ratings exist
at 4.6+.

| When | Action | Confirm before moving |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T-1 | Stage ebook price at $0.99 in KDP dashboard | Paperback listing is otherwise complete |
| T0 | Confirm $0.99 is live; enrol in KDP Select (90 days) | Cover, categories, keywords all locked (see calendar.md T-7/T-10 rows) |
| T+7 | Move ebook price to $4.99 | Launch-week velocity checked; if it stalled, the Select free day (T+4) should already have run |
| T+30 | Check total ratings count | 25+ at 4.6 or better -> move to $9.99. Short of that -> hold at $4.99, recheck weekly, do not force the move early |
| T+90 (beyond this calendar) | Reassess KDP Select re-enrollment | Decide based on 90 days of real KU-read and rank data, not a guess |

**Never**: a coupon code to an aggregator site, a price drop timed to chase
a competitor, or moving to $9.99 before the 25-rating threshold just
because 30 days have passed. The threshold is ratings, not days.
