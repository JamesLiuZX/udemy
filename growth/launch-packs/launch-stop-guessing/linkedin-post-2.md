sounds fine until you chain ten steps together. Then it's a coin flip.

Multiply the per-step success rate across the chain and the compounding
catches almost everyone off guard the first time they actually do the
maths on a workflow they've already shipped.

week. The chapter on agent reliability doesn't tell you agents are

to put it.
