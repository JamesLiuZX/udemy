An AI feature is a probability distribution, not a function. You cannot
write acceptance criteria for it the way you write them for a button.

That's the single idea the whole course is built around, and it's the
free preview lecture (0.3) if you want to see the teaching before you
commit to anything.

Practically, it means the acceptance criteria you already know how to
write ("click submit, see confirmation") don't transfer. The fix isn't a
longer checklist. It's a different kind of criteria: evaluation
thresholds on a golden dataset. "94% pass rate across the forty hardest
cases we could find" instead of "it seems to work."

Section 4 of the course builds that golden set from scratch, then gets
two reviewers to agree on a rubric within a calibration protocol tight
enough to actually hold up in a stakeholder review.
