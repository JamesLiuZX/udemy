Most products marketed as "AI agents" this year are workflows with a
model step, not genuine agents. That distinction matters more than it
sounds, because scoping the two correctly is the difference between a
feature that fails safely and one that doesn't.

Section 6 of AI Product Skills for PMs & Analysts covers the reliability
maths that actually predicts where an agent breaks: chain enough steps
together at a 95% per-step success rate and you're at a coin flip by step
ten. Most teams don't do that arithmetic until after the incident that
makes them do it.

This isn't a tool tour. It's not a prompt library either. What this
course is not, said plainly, is a course you could replace with an
afternoon of ChatGPT. It's process and judgement, taught through work you
actually do: an AI Feature PRD, a golden dataset, a cost model, a risk
register, an agent scoping worksheet, a metric tree for the numbers that
matter after launch.

Twelve artefacts, no coding required beyond one line-by-line-explained
Python lecture. Launch pricing runs through this week.
