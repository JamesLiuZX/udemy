> AI Features Are Distributions, Not Functions (Free Lecture)

**Description:**
```
Every hard argument you'll have about an AI feature's readiness comes
back to one wrong assumption: that it behaves like the software you've
shipped your whole career.

Deterministic software gives the same output for the same input, every
time. An AI feature gives you a sample from a range of possible outputs,
and the range is the actual product. This free lecture is the idea the

a spreadsheet and in 30 lines of Python, designing and defending a RAG

required.

**Description:**
```
Twelve ad variants from one brief, generated the same day, by the same
system. Eight lose money outright. Three are noise. One is a real
winner, and nobody could have told you which one in advance by looking
