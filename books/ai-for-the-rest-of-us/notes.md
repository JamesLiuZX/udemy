## Register, distinct from the other two books

This is the warmest, least jargon-tolerant of the three books running so
far. stop-guessing assumes a PM vocabulary; ai-employee assumes a general
office-work reader; this one assumes no professional context at all, a
reader who might be retired, might be a parent, might have picked this up
because their kid told them to. Concretely:

- No business examples as the default case. Use household, family, and
  personal-admin examples throughout.
- Sentences even shorter than ai-employee's. If a sentence needs a comma
  to hold a subordinate clause, ask whether it should just be two
  sentences instead.
- Never assume the reader has used AI tools before, but never talk down
  to them either. The tone is a knowledgeable friend, not a manual.
- Chapter 06 (doctor's office) and chapter 09 (scams) both touch health
  and safety. Be explicit every time: this helps you prepare questions
  and understand terms, it is not a diagnosis and does not replace a
  professional. Say it in-chapter, not just as a disclaimer page.
