## A section heading

Sections within a chapter use a second-level heading. They should be rare;
if a chapter needs more than two or three, it is probably two chapters
wearing one title.

*Italic* marks emphasis, not a defined term; **bold** marks a term being
defined for the first time. Mixing the two conventions inside one chapter
is the kind of small inconsistency a reader feels without being able to
name it.

### A subsection, used sparingly

Subsections exist for the rare case where a section has real internal
structure worth signposting. Most chapters in a well-edited book never
need one.
