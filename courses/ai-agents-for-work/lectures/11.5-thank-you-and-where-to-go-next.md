## You did not just watch four agents get built. You built one yourself.

lead: Priya's week was the practice. Your capstone was the point.

Look back at where this started. An inbox with forty unread emails and a
missed renewal. You now have a working method for turning a task like

it shouldn't.

## The five ideas underneath everything you built

1. Write the job description before you touch a canvas
2. Judgement is the hard part. Triggers and routing are the easy part
3. Trial before trust, every time, no exceptions
4. Draft-only until an agent has earned the right to send
5. The honest number beats the impressive one

@narrate
[INSTRUCTOR-INPUT: pick one of these five that mattered most to you while
