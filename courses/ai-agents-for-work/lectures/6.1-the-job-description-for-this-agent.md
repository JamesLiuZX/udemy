@slide quote
kicker: The scene this agent fixes
> I meant to chase that. I just forgot, and now it's two days overdue.

attrib: Every client-facing job, at least once a quarter

@narrate
Priya's renewals do not fail because she is careless. They slip because
a hundred small tasks compete for the same Tuesday, and a follow up due
in eight days loses to whatever is due today. This agent's entire job is
noticing the eight-day ones before they become today's problem, and
handing you a draft instead of a reason to feel behind.


@slide example
kicker: This agent's job description
::: example Filled from the C05 template
**The job**: drafts a chase email for any open renewal within three
weeks of its date.
**Scope**: reads the renewal pipeline. Writes one Gmail draft per
client that needs one.
**Action**: draft only. Full stop. No send action exists anywhere in
this build.
**Ceiling**: never mentions a penalty, a threat, or the premium amount.
Polite, factual, nothing else.
**Trial task**: two weeks, every single draft read by Priya before it
is ever sent by hand.
:::

@narrate
Read the action line again, because it is written differently from the
first three job descriptions on purpose. It does not say "mostly draft"
or "draft unless." It says draft only, full stop, and the build in this
section is going to prove that sentence is true in your wiring, not
just on paper. Write your own version of it before you open n8n.


@slide callout
kicker: Why this is the design centre, not an afterthought
::: callout Draft-only isn't a safety feature bolted on at the end
You are going to build this constraint into the very first version of
this agent, the same way you would build a lock into a door while you
are still framing the house. Section 8 tells you exactly what happens
when a constraint like this gets added after something already went
wrong instead of before.
:::
