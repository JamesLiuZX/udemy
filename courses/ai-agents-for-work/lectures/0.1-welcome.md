@slide title
## AI Agents for Your Job
kicker: Welcome

@narrate
Hello, and welcome. By the end of this course you will have built four
working agents: one that triages your inbox, one that turns a meeting
recording into action items, one that writes your weekly report, and one
that chases the follow ups you keep meaning to send.

I've spent years building AI products in Silicon Valley and at major
technology companies, including ByteDance, and the pattern that shows up in
every one of those jobs is the same one you'll meet in your own inbox this
week: the boring, repeatable part of the work is exactly what a properly
supervised agent is good at, and exactly where people either save real hours
or make a mess trying.

Every agent gets built screen by screen, in a free tool called n8n, and
every one of them comes home with you as a template you can open and adapt
in minutes. There is no code anywhere in this course.


@slide bullets
kicker: What you will have by Friday
## Four agents, ten artifacts

1. An inbox triage agent that catches the email you would have missed
2. A meeting notes agent that turns a call into action items nobody chases you for
3. A weekly report agent that drafts the update your manager actually reads
4. A follow up agent that never lets a chase slip, and never sends without you

@narrate
Each agent ships as a downloadable workflow you import in a couple of
clicks. Alongside them you get six more artifacts: a job description
template for onboarding an agent properly, a trial task checklist, a spot
check rubric, a guardrails worksheet, a cost tracker, and a one page plan
for rolling this out to your team.

Ten artifacts in total. You will use the first one before this section
ends.

Threaded through the whole course is one running case. Priya Malhotra
manages renewals at Calloway and Finch, a commercial insurance brokerage:
one hundred and eighty staff, three regional offices, and not one engineer
on the payroll. Priya looks after forty one accounts on her own, and you
will watch her onboard all four of these agents against her real week,
mistake by mistake, save by save. She is not the expert in this story.
She is you, a few weeks from now.


@slide statement
class: center
kicker: The one thing to hold onto
## An agent is an employee, not an app.

lead: You do not install it. You onboard it.

@narrate
That is the whole course in one sentence, and we will come back to it
constantly. You would never hand a brand new hire your whole inbox on
their first morning. The same caution applies here, and it is the
difference between people who get real hours back and people who spend a
weekend cleaning up after a mistake.

Two promises before we start. First, every technique in this course
names its cost: the checking time, the ways each agent fails, the tasks
that should never be handed over at all. If a lecture ever sounds like a
sales page, I have failed. Second, nothing here asks you to become
technical. Priya never writes a line of code in this story. Neither will
you.

I built this for the person who owns Section 0's problem for real: forty
unread emails, a report due Friday, and no budget for an engineer to fix any
of it. If that's your week, keep watching.

See you in the next lecture. We are building your first agent in the next
twenty minutes.
