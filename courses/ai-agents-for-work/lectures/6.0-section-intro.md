## Agent #4: Follow-Ups That Never Slip
lead: And never, under any circumstance, send on their own.

@narrate
You've probably had a message go out that shouldn't have: an email sent
before it was checked, a reply-all that couldn't be unsent. Hold that
feeling. It's the entire design brief for this section.

The last agent in this course chases the follow ups Priya would
otherwise forget. It is also the one built around a single non-negotiable
rule, and this section exists to show you exactly how that rule gets
enforced in the build itself, not just promised in a job description.
