@slide statement
kicker: The last step, third time round
## Unpin the test data. Connect your real sheet. Let Monday's schedule do the rest.

lead: One new thing this time: nobody has to remember to run it.

@narrate
Open your Google Sheets node, unpin the test data, and connect your
real credential. Point Document and Sheet at your actual pipeline. The
schedule you set in Section 5.2 means this now runs itself, every
Monday, whether or not anyone thinks to trigger it. That is the whole
point of building it this way: the twenty minutes it saves you happens
whether or not you remember it exists.


@slide bullets
kicker: A short checklist before you rely on it
## The same caution, aimed at a clock this time

1. Trigger a manual run first and read the report against your own count
2. Leave it on Trial. Three Mondays before you stop checking every number
3. Confirm the schedule's time zone matches yours, not the server's default

@narrate
That third point is an easy, boring mistake with a real cost: a report
that lands at 2am your time because nobody checked the time zone setting
is a report nobody reads before their actual Monday meeting.
