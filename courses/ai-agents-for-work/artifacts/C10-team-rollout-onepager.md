# Team Rollout One-Pager

*From AI Agents for Your Job. One page. Built from your C09 numbers. This is the document you actually hand your manager or your team, not a slide deck.*

## What this is

One sentence. What you built, for whom, and what it does.

> Example: "Four agents that triage my inbox, turn meeting calls into
> action items, draft my weekly renewal report, and chase overdue
> documents, all draft-only until I review them."

## The numbers, straight from C09

- Hours saved a week, cleanly counted: __________
- AI cost per month, all agents combined: __________
- How long each agent spent on Trial before you trusted it: __________

## The safeguards, named plainly

- Every agent's job description, scope and ceiling (link or attach C05s)
- The trial and spot-check discipline that caught real mistakes (reference C06/C07)
- The one incident, if you want to include it: what nearly went wrong, and what stopped it

## What you are asking for

Be specific. Not "can I use AI more," but the actual next step.

> Example: "I'd like to share these four templates with the team, with
> the same job-description and trial process attached, starting with
> whoever wants to try one."

## What you are not asking for

State the boundary as clearly as the ask. This is what makes the pitch credible rather than hopeful.

> Example: "I'm not asking to change how sign-off works. That decision
> stays exactly where it is."

## For the team, not just the boss

If teammates are adopting these templates:

- [ ] Each one runs their own Trial, even on a "proven" template. Section 10.4 explains why this stays mandatory.
- [ ] Each one fills in C05 for their own scope, not a copy of yours
- [ ] Spot checks (C07) continue after trust is granted, for everyone, indefinitely
