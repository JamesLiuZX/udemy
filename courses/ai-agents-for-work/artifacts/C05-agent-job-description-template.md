## 1. The job, in one sentence
What is this agent actually for. Write it the way you would describe a new
hire's role to their own manager, not the way you would describe a feature
to an engineer.

> Example: "Reads my inbox each morning and flags anything from a client
> that needs a same-day reply."

## 3. What it can do (action level)
Circle one.

- **Read only** — surfaces information, changes nothing
- **Draft only** — prepares a message or document, a human sends it
- **Act** — sends, updates or files something without a human step

## 4. The ceiling (blast radius)
A number, value or condition it may never cross without a human.

> Example: "Never drafts a chase for an amount over $2,000 without flagging
> it to me first."

## 5. What "urgent" or "correct" means, in this job specifically
Generic instructions produce generic mistakes. Give it the actual judgement
call a person in this role would make.

> Example: "Urgent means the client names a deadline, or the subject
> mentions a payment, refund or cancellation."
