## Pre-Flight QC
lead: Catch the unshippable ad before it spends money or triggers a policy strike.

@narrate
One draft in Harlan's own batch claimed the socks were clinically proven
to reduce swelling by forty percent. Nobody had ever tested that number.
Dana would never have signed her name to it. It never ran, because
someone asked one question before it went anywhere near an ad account:
could you defend this out loud?

Every section so far has been about making more, faster: more angles,
more statics, more video, tuned for more placements. This section is the
brake pedal, and it belongs here, before Section seven's testing, not
after. That's what it actually builds: the pass that catches a draft
like that one before a dollar gets spent on it.
