## Sam ran Harlan's strongest cut, unchanged, on Meta and on TikTok the same week. It won on one. It lost on the other.
lead: Same creative, five placements. This section is the discipline that explains the gap.

@narrate
On Meta, the clinical-authority cut won as filmed. On TikTok, that same
cut barely registered, and the raw, unframed 12-hour shift cut won there
instead. Same product, same claims boundary, two different answers to
what works here.

Nothing ahead asks you to make anything new. Every ad you've already
built stays exactly as it is. What changes is where it runs and how it's
cut for that placement, because a feed and a For You page don't reward
the same fifteen seconds for the same reasons.
