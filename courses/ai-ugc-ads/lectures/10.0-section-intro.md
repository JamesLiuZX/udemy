## Doing the Job
lead: Convert the skill into income, in-house or as a service.

@narrate
Everything up to this point answers "can you run this system." This
section answers a different question: does anyone actually pay you for
it. Those are not the same skill, and a lot of genuinely capable people
never close that gap.

Sam's own path through this course is the worked example here. Fifteen
hours a week on one account became a system worth pitching, and the
same honest reporting Section 9.5 built is what makes that pitch
credible instead of just a claim.
