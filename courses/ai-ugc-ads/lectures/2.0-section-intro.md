## Sam Asked for "Brand Voice." Dana Sent Him Three Numbers Instead.
lead: Generation quality gets decided in that exchange, before Sam ever opens a generator.

@narrate
On a Sunday night, Sam texted Dana asking for the usual: brand voice notes,
so he could start briefing the batch. Dana didn't send tone words. She sent
her price, her margin, and the one claim she will not let an ad make, no
matter how well it tests.

That's the whole design of this section, stated as one exchange. Section
one gave you a number: around twenty variants to find a real winner. This
section makes sure those twenty are actually twenty different bets, not one
idea wearing twenty outfits.

Everything here happens before a generator opens. The product truth, the
awareness levels, the hooks, the angle map, the landing page: it all has to
match. Skip this section and generation still works. It just produces
twenty confident variations of the same narrow guess.

## Five documents, then the workshop that combines them

1. Product truth: the facts and the claims boundary, agreed before you write a word of copy
2. Awareness levels: matching the angle to what the audience already believes
3. The hook taxonomy: named patterns for the first half second
4. The angle map: one product truth doc to twenty testable angles
5. Landing congruence: why a great ad still dies on the wrong page

@narrate
Five pieces, building toward the workshop where you write a real brief.

Product truth comes first, because every claim your ads make has to trace
back to something true and something Dana can defend. Awareness levels and
the hook taxonomy give you the actual vocabulary for a testable angle,
instead of "make it punchier." The angle map turns all of it into twenty
real bets. And landing congruence closes the loop: the best hook in the
world still loses if the page it lands on breaks the promise the ad made.

Before the next lecture, open a blank note. Write down your own price, what
it actually costs you to deliver, and the one line about your product you
would not want to defend under bright lights. That's the first three lines
of your own product truth document, and it is exactly what the workshop in
2.6 turns into artifact B01.
