## The Static Image System
lead: One brief to twenty on-brand statics, without the AI-image tells.

@narrate
Picture Sam opening the batch folder on a Wednesday afternoon: twenty
statics (a static is one still-image ad, one picture, one message) sitting
there, generated overnight from the angle map. At a glance the batch looks
finished. It isn't quite.

I'm not going to pretend the first batch comes out clean, because Sam's
didn't. This section spends as much time on what goes wrong, colour drift,
mangled text, a product that doesn't quite look like the product, as it
does on the generation itself. Both halves matter equally.

@slide bullets
kicker: What's ahead
## Generation, then everything that catches what generation misses

1. Where image generation actually stands right now, honestly assessed
2. Batch generation: turning twenty angles into twenty real statics at once
3. Brand control: checking a generated image against your real colours, logo and product
4. Text on image: where words rendered straight into the picture still break, and the workaround
5. Packshots (clean product shots) and lifestyle scenes (product in a moment), without a shoot

@narrate
Five lectures, and notice the shape: one on what these tools can and can't
do honestly, one on the actual batch, and three on the specific ways a
batch goes wrong and how to catch it.

That balance is deliberate. A course that only shows the wins teaches you
to trust a generator more than you should. This one shows you Harlan's
first batch failing in two specific, ordinary ways, because that's what
actually happens, and knowing the failure in advance is most of the
defence against it. Before the next lecture, if you already have a batch
of your own sitting somewhere, open it and just look. You'll want that
image fresh in mind for what comes next.
