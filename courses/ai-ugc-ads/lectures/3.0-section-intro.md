## The Static Image System
lead: One brief to twenty on-brand statics, without the AI-image tells.

@narrate
The brief from Section two is finished. This section is where it finally
becomes pixels: twenty statics, generated from the angle map, ready to
test.

I'm not going to pretend the first batch comes out clean, because it
doesn't. This section spends as much time on what goes wrong, colour
drift, mangled text, a product that doesn't quite look like the product, as
it does on the generation itself. Both halves matter equally.

## Generation, then everything that catches what generation misses

1. Where image generation actually stands right now, honestly assessed
2. Batch generation: turning twenty angles into twenty real statics
3. Brand control: keeping the product, and any face, actually accurate
4. Text on image: where it still breaks, and the workaround
5. Packshots and lifestyle scenes, without a product shoot

@narrate
Five lectures, and notice the shape: one on what these tools can and can't
do honestly, one on the actual batch, and three on the specific ways a
batch goes wrong and how to catch it.

That balance is deliberate. A course that only shows the wins teaches you
to trust a generator more than you should. This one shows you Harlan's
first batch failing in two specific, ordinary ways, because that's what
actually happens, and knowing the failure in advance is most of the
defence against it.
