## The Testing Machine
lead: The ad account is the instrument. Build it before you spend.

@narrate
Every lecture up to this point has been about making a batch worth
testing: briefed properly, generated at volume, checked for every way it
could fail. None of that answers the actual question this course opened
with. Which one wins.

This section builds the thing that actually answers it: budgets and kill
rules decided before the data arrives, a naming system that survives six
months, and the statistical honesty to read a small-spend result without
fooling yourself. Harlan's own testing architecture is the worked
example throughout.

@narrate
Four lectures, then a workshop where you build your own testing
