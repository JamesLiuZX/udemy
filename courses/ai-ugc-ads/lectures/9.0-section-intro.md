## Scale Ops
lead: From one scored test to a weekly machine.

@narrate
You ended Section eight with a single ad set, properly read: winner,
loser, or noise, honestly labelled. That's not yet an operation. It's
one result, on one day, and nothing here yet guarantees it happens
again next week.

This section builds the machine around that habit for you: a weekly
sprint that keeps fresh angles ready before fatigue forces the issue, a
real look at what generation actually costs against your media spend,
and the reporting that shows your whole system's work to whoever's
paying for it. Harlan's account, now scaling past its first test, is
the worked example throughout.
