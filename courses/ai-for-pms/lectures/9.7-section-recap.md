## You can now measure whether a feature matters, not just whether it works.
lead: Seven lectures ago, a golden set was the only measurement you had. Here's the honest tally of what you can measure now.

@narrate
Seven lectures ago, this section opened with the gap between a golden
set and a real user: one tells you the feature works, the other tells
you whether anyone actually wants it. Here's what closes that gap now.

You've got three metrics built specifically for a feature that behaves
differently every time it runs, instead of borrowed engagement numbers
that can quietly mean the opposite of what they look like. You know how
to instrument before launch so those numbers are answerable on day one,
and how to A/B test something that isn't deterministic without a
standard calculator quietly misleading you. You've got the discipline
that actually closes a feedback loop instead of just logging one, and a
leadership dashboard honest enough to show a bad number.
