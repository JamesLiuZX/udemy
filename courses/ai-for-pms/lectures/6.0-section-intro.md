## Agents and Automation
lead: The riskiest branch on Section two's decision tree, unpacked into the judgement that keeps it from becoming an incident.

@narrate
Back in Section two, the decision tree gave agent one box among four, and
moved on. That was deliberate. You needed the other three first, because
an agent is what you reach for once prompting, retrieval, and fine-tuning
have all genuinely fallen short.

This section is that fourth box, opened up properly. By the end, you'll
know what actually separates an agent from a chatbot with extra steps,
the maths that tells you when a five-step plan is quietly a coin flip,
and how to scope one so a failure costs you an afternoon instead of a
