## Speaking Engineer
lead: Twenty phrases and one diagram, earning you credibility in a conversation, not a certification.

@narrate
This is the shortest section in the course, on purpose. You don't need
to write code or pass an exam. You need to hold your own for ten minutes
in a room with an engineer who's deciding whether to take your spec
seriously.

That's a narrower goal than it sounds, and it's achievable in under an
hour of lectures.

## The whole stack, then the vocabulary for it

1. The AI stack, in one diagram you can redraw from memory
2. Prompt versus RAG versus fine-tune versus agent, and when each wins
3. Embeddings and vector search, without the underlying maths
4. Latency, throughput, and reading a benchmark table critically
5. Twenty phrases that make an engineer trust you in five minutes

@narrate
Five things, and they build on each other in order.

You'll get the whole stack on one diagram, the actual decision tree
between a prompt, retrieval, a fine-tune, and an agent, and enough of
embeddings and vector search to follow a real conversation without the
underlying mathematics.

Then latency, throughput, and how to read a benchmark table the way an
engineer already does. It closes with twenty specific phrases, the ones
that signal you've done the reading, in the first five minutes rather
than the last.
